Before you begin

  1. Every item is multiple choice with one correct answer. Unanswered items are marked wrong, so answer everything.
  2. The timer starts when you press Start Attempt and keeps running even if you close or refresh the page.
  3. Starting an attempt uses it, even if you leave before submitting. Your unfinished attempt resumes when you return.
  4. Use Previous, Next or the question palette to move around. You can flag items to come back to.
  5. When time runs out, your exam is submitted automatically.
  6. Correct answers and explanations appear only after you submit.
